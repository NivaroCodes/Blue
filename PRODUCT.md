## Platform

## Users
Analysts reviewing possible financial pyramid signals in Telegram and WhatsApp messages. SafeNet KZ track 3; frontend member of a three-person team.

## Product Purpose
Prioritize messages, inspect exact evidence and context, and record a human review. A risk signal is not a legal verdict.

## Confirmed constraints
- Russian/Kazakh/transliterated content. Open, synthetic or anonymized data only.
- Preserve risk filters, source filters, search, pagination, export, review notes, live polling, API settings and direct detail URLs.
- React/TypeScript/Vite; existing Zod API contract in docs/API.md remains compatible.
- Demo scores are authored examples, not real AI results. Do not invent evaluation metrics or cross-account relationships.
- Product name is Blue, replacing Kraken by the user's current instruction.
- User delegates architecture and visual decisions and requests animation, 3D and a generated preview before frontend implementation.
- Maintain reduced motion, keyboard access, mobile layouts and no-WebGL fallback.
